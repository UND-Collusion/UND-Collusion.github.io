UND COLLUSION CONSTITUTION

1.	League Officers
-	League Commissioner: Darrick Richardson        Assistant to the Commissioner: Dale

2.	League Setup
-	# of Owners: 12
-	# of Active Roster Spots: 25
-	# of Taxi Squad Spots: 2
-	# of IR Spots: 2
-	Annual FAAB Budget: $250
-	Offseason FAAB (end of season – Completion of rookie draft) : $100

Roster Format: QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, Flex, Flex, Superflex, Bench (16), Taxi (2)

3.	Annual League Dues: $130 per Team/Owner
a.	IF YOU TRADE A FUTURE PICK, YOU ARE LIABLE FOR THE NEXT SEASONS ENTRY FEE

4.	Payout Structure
-	1st : $700
-	2nd : $358
-	3rd : $128
-	Weekly Bonuses: $350
-	Trophy Name Plate + Domain Name for League website = $24

Weekly Bonus: During each week of the regular season (1-14), the team that scores the most points wins the weekly bonus of $25. Which will be paid out at the end of the season

4.	Playoff Configuration
There will be a 6-team championship playoff each year starting on week 15. Playoffs shall consist of the top 6 seeds, with the bye week being awarded to the top 2 seeds. 

Playoff Seeding Tiebreakers:
1.	Overall Record
2.	Points For (PF)
3.	Points Against (PA) 

Round 1 (Week 15): 3 seed vs 6 seed; 4 seed vs 5 seed; Bye:1 Seed and 2 Seed
Round 2 (Week 16): 1st seed vs lowest remaining seed; 2nd seed vs highest remaining seed
o	The two losers of the round 1 matchup will face each other, winner will be awarded the 7th Pick in the draft, Loser gets 8th
Round 3 (Week 17): Two winners from the round 2 matchups play in the championship
o	The two losers of the round 2 matchups will face each other, winner will be awarded the 9th pick in the draft, loser gets 10th.

5.	Schedule
a.	Every team will play each other once (Weeks 1-11)
b.	Weeks 12-14 will be a round robin style
i.	1st-4th will play each other to finish the season
ii.	5th-8th will play each other to finish the season
iii.	9th-12th will play each other to finish the season


6.	Compensation Pick Playoff – “Toilet Bowl”
The bottom 6 teams will play in a playoff each year for a compensatory draft pick. The pick that will be granted to the winner of the toilet bowl will be draft pick # 1.13.  

Round 1 (Week 15): 12 seed vs 9 seed; 10 seed vs 11 seed; Bye: 7 Seed and 8 Seed
Round 2 (Week 16): 1st seed vs lowest remaining seed; 2nd seed vs highest remaining seed
Round 3 (Week 17): Two winners from the round 2 matchups play in the championship

7.	Rookie Draft Order Determination – Picks 1-6
-	Rookie draft order for picks 1-6 are determined after week 13. The picks are determined by the following criteria:
1.	Overall Record
2.	Points For Inverse order.
a.	Max PF will be used if a owner fails to start a competitive lineup under the discretion of the Commissioner
b.	In the 1% chance there is a tie, it will be broken with a Coin Flip. Best of 7. 

8.	Taxi Squad
-	2 Taxi Squad Slots
-	Rookies only
-	Players must be in the Taxi spots in Sleeper before the first game of the year begins. 
-	If a player is removed from the taxi squad at any time during the season, the player is no longer eligible to be put back on to the taxi squad and the taxi slot will remain vacant until the next season.
-	If a taxi squad player is traded, the team receiving the taxi squad player must place that player on the active 25-man roster. 
-	Once a player is no longer a rookie (their sophomore year, defined as starting the day of the next league’s rookie draft day), they must be removed from the taxi squad (drop, or place on active roster).

9.	Important Deadlines

-	Trade Deadline – 11 PM CST Wednesday evening of Week 15 (Rd 1 of playoffs)
-	Taxi Squad Deadline – Beginning of the first NFL regular season game
-	Active Roster Cut Down to 25 (if applicable) - Beginning of the first NFL regular season game
-	Pre Rookie Draft Roster Cut Down to 23 – 1 Week before Rookie Draft

10.	General
-	Starting Injured players or players on bye weeks (when other roster options are available) is subject to penalty. Repeat offenders will be subject to penalty that will be voted on by the league. 
-	TANKING – Owners must start players that are active on game day. Benching studs in an attempt at tanking is subject to draft pick penalty as voted on by the league.
